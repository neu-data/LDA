# Module 7 glossary input

- **Generalized estimating equations (GEE):** a method that estimates the coefficients of a marginal mean model by solving estimating equations that weight each cluster's residuals by a working covariance, with standard errors from the sandwich estimator.
- **Marginal model:** a model for the population mean of the outcome given covariates, E(Y | x), without conditioning on participant-specific random effects.
- **Population-average (marginal) effect:** an effect that compares mean outcomes (proportions, mean counts) between populations defined by covariate values.
- **Cluster:** the independent unit in a GEE analysis; in longitudinal data, a participant and all of their observations.
- **Estimating equation:** an equation, such as the sum over clusters of D' V^-1 (y - mu) = 0, whose solution defines the estimator; it need not come from a likelihood.
- **Working correlation:** the analyst's assumed correlation matrix for observations within a cluster in GEE (independence, exchangeable, AR(1), unstructured); it affects efficiency but not the consistency of the estimates.
- **Exchangeable working correlation:** equal correlation between every pair of observations within a cluster.
- **Robust (sandwich) standard error:** a standard error from B^-1 M B^-1, where the "meat" M uses the empirical outer products of cluster residuals; valid whatever the true correlation when there are many clusters.
- **Model-based (naive) standard error:** a standard error that assumes the working covariance is correct; in geepack stored in `fit$geese$vbeta.naiv`.
- **Scale (dispersion) parameter in GEE:** the multiplier phi of the variance function; for a Poisson GEE a value above 1 indicates overdispersion.
- **Mancl-DeRouen correction:** a small-sample bias correction of the sandwich that inflates each cluster's residuals by the inverse of one minus its leverage; for a linear model it equals the leave-one-cluster-out jackknife variance.
- **QIC / CIC:** quasi-likelihood information criterion for GEE and its penalty term; QIC is a weak guide for choosing the working correlation.
- **Inverse-probability weighting (IPW):** weighting each observed outcome by the inverse of its estimated probability of being observed (for dropout, of remaining in the study up to that visit), so that observed data represent the full population under MAR.
- **Weighted GEE:** GEE with inverse-probability weights, normally with an independence working correlation; valid under MAR if the dropout model is correct.
- **Positivity:** the requirement that every participant has a non-negligible probability of being observed, so that weights stay bounded.
