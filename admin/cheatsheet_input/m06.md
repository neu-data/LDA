# Module 6 cheat-sheet input: interpretation, contrasts and prediction

## Interpreting coefficients with interactions and centring

- **Purpose:** read each coefficient as the answer to the right question.
- **Key assumptions:** treatment coding of factors; known origin of every continuous variable.
- **R example:**
  ```r
  walk$month12 <- walk$month - 12
  lmer(sbp ~ month12 * arm + (1 + month12 | id), data = walk, control = lmer_ctrl)
  # armSP now = SP - UC difference at month 12; slopes unchanged
  fixef(m_prim)["visit_f12:armSP"]   # = difference in mean change to month 12
  ```
- **Report:** the coding of time and covariates (age centred at 60, BMI at 29), and which coefficient or contrast is the estimand.
- **Common error:** reporting `armSP` from an interaction model as "the treatment effect" (it is the difference at time 0).

## Estimated marginal means (EMMs)

- **Purpose:** model-based mean outcome for each arm at each visit, using all data (valid under MAR).
- **Key assumptions:** those of the model; covariates set to reference values (numeric at means, factors equally weighted by default).
- **R example:**
  ```r
  emm <- emmeans(m_prim, ~ visit_f * arm)     # Satterthwaite df (set in setup.R)
  summary(emm)
  ```
- **Report:** EMMs with 95% CIs, stating the model and degrees-of-freedom method.
- **Common error:** reporting raw observed means as model estimates (in WALK they are 0.5 to 1 mmHg lower at month 12 because high-SBP participants dropped out).

## Planned contrasts: difference in mean change

- **Purpose:** estimate the primary estimand, (mu_a,12 - mu_a,0) - (mu_UC,12 - mu_UC,0).
- **Key assumptions:** as for EMMs; weights sum to zero and are placed in the right cells.
- **R example:**
  ```r
  cells <- expand.grid(visit_f = levels(walk$visit_f), arm = levels(walk$arm))
  w <- function(a, v) as.numeric(cells$arm == a & cells$visit_f == v)
  chg <- function(a) w(a, "12") - w(a, "0")
  contrast(emm, list("EP - UC" = chg("EP") - chg("UC")), infer = TRUE)
  contrast(emm, interaction = c(visit_f = "trt.vs.ctrl1", arm = "trt.vs.ctrl1"))
  ```
- **Report:** within-arm changes and differences with 95% CIs and p-values (WALK: SP - UC -5.4 (-8.2 to -2.6), EP - UC -9.0 (-11.8 to -6.1) mmHg).
- **Common error:** building the weight vector in a different cell order from the `emmeans` grid; forgetting that `"trt.vs.ctrl"` applies a Dunnett adjustment by default.

## Multiplicity for prespecified comparisons

- **Purpose:** control the familywise error rate for the family of confirmatory claims.
- **Key assumptions:** family and method defined before seeing the data.
- **R example:**
  ```r
  contrast(emm, L_primary, adjust = "mvt")    # Dunnett (exact)
  contrast(emm, L_primary, adjust = "holm")   # Holm (Bonferroni intervals)
  ```
- **Report:** the primary family, the adjustment method, adjusted p-values and simultaneous CIs; secondary comparisons with unadjusted CIs, labelled as secondary.
- **Common error:** adjusting every comparison (loses power) or none while making several confirmatory claims.

## Population-average vs subject-specific predictions

- **Purpose:** predict for the population (fixed effects) or for a known participant (fixed effects + BLUPs).
- **Key assumptions:** identity link (the two coincide on average); random-effects distribution correct for subject-specific predictions.
- **R example:**
  ```r
  predict(m_prim, re.form = NA)   # population average
  predict(m_prim)                  # subject specific
  ```
- **Report:** which kind of prediction is shown.
- **Common error:** averaging `predict(m)` over observed rows and calling the result estimated means (it tracks who stayed).

## Confidence vs prediction intervals

- **Purpose:** uncertainty about a mean (CI) versus the range of a single new measurement (PI).
- **Key assumptions:** normal random effects and residuals; variance components treated as known.
- **R example:**
  ```r
  G <- as.matrix(VarCorr(m_prim)$id); s2 <- sigma(m_prim)^2; z <- c(1, 12)
  se_new <- sqrt(SE_mean^2 + t(z) %*% G %*% z + s2)   # new participant
  # existing participant: replace G by the condVar of the BLUPs and add z'b_i
  ```
- **Report:** both, labelled (WALK EP month 12: CI width about 5 mmHg, PI width about 54 mmHg).
- **Common error:** presenting a confidence band as the range of individual values.

## Presenting fitted trajectories

- **Purpose:** honest, interpretable figures and tables.
- **Key points:** model-estimated means with named 95% bands; observed means overlaid; real time on the x-axis; labelled axes with units; consistent arm colours; no cropped or dual axes; table with estimates and CIs before p-values.
- **R example:**
  ```r
  ggplot(emm_tab, aes(month, emmean, colour = arm, fill = arm)) +
    geom_ribbon(aes(ymin = lower.CL, ymax = upper.CL), alpha = 0.15, colour = NA) +
    geom_line() + scale_colour_arm() + scale_fill_arm()
  ```
- **Common error:** equally spaced categorical visits on the x-axis when the schedule is unequal.

## Decision points (for method-selection diagrams)

- Estimand is a difference in change? Contrast of EMMs with explicit weights (or the month-v interaction coefficient in the saturated cell-means model).
- Several confirmatory comparisons sharing a control? Dunnett, Holm or a fixed sequence, prespecified. Secondary or descriptive? No adjustment, clearly labelled.
- Question about the average participant? Population-average prediction with CI. About one participant? Subject-specific prediction with PI.
- Randomised trial with pre-randomisation baseline in the outcome vector? Constrained baseline (cLDA) is an option; not for observational comparisons.
